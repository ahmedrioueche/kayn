# Product Specification — KAYN

## Objective
Create a premium, responsive Algerian car-rental landing page.

The visitor should quickly understand KAYN, browse featured vehicles, compare essential specs/prices, see service locations, understand the process, and contact the business.

## Required sections

### Header
- KAYN wordmark
- Accueil
- Véhicules
- Comment ça marche
- Villes
- Avis
- Primary CTA: Réserver
- Mobile menu

### Hero
Eyebrow: LOCATION DE VOITURES • ALGÉRIE

Headline: "Votre prochaine route commence ici."

Body: "Louez une voiture fiable, propre et adaptée à vos besoins, simplement."

CTAs:
- Voir les véhicules
- Réserver sur WhatsApp

Include one strong vehicle image and a floating booking-style visual card with Ville, Date de départ, Date de retour, CTA.

The card is visual in the MVP and must not falsely claim real availability checking.

### Trust strip
- Véhicules récents
- Prix transparents
- Réservation rapide
- Assistance 7j/7

These are demo positioning claims and should not be represented as independently verified facts.

### Featured vehicles
Peugeot 208, Renault Clio, Volkswagen Golf 8, Toyota Yaris.

Each card:
- image
- model
- category
- transmission
- fuel
- seats
- sample daily price
- CTA

### Categories
- Économique
- Berline
- SUV
- Premium

### How it works
1. Choisissez votre véhicule.
2. Choisissez vos dates.
3. Contactez-nous.
4. Récupérez votre voiture.

### Locations
- Alger
- Oran
- Constantine
- Blida

### Why KAYN
- Véhicules soigneusement sélectionnés
- Processus simple
- Tarifs clairs
- Service client disponible

### Testimonials
Three concise sample reviews with Algerian names/cities. Store them as demo content.

### Final CTA
"Besoin d'une voiture ?"

"Choisissez votre véhicule et contactez-nous pour vérifier sa disponibilité."

CTA: "Réserver maintenant"

### Footer
Brand, short description, navigation, cities, WhatsApp placeholder, Instagram placeholder, Alger, Algeria, copyright.

## Functional requirements
- Section navigation works.
- Mobile menu works.
- CTA links use configurable URLs.
- Vehicle cards are responsive.
- Hover/focus states work.
- No fake real-time availability.
- No backend required.

## Accessibility
Semantic HTML, logical headings, keyboard navigation, focus-visible states, meaningful alt text, adequate contrast, reduced-motion support.

## Non-goals
No authentication, payment, database, admin, customer accounts, real-time availability, booking engine, fleet management, or complex API-backed filtering.
