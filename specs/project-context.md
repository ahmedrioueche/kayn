# Project Context — KAYN

## Brand
Name: KAYN

Type: Premium car rental / location de voitures

Market: Algeria, initially focused on Alger, Oran, Constantine, and Blida.

## Positioning
KAYN is a modern, trustworthy car-rental brand for Algerian customers who want a clean, reliable vehicle and a simple reservation experience.

The website should look substantially more polished than a typical local car-rental website while remaining practical and familiar.

## Audience
- People travelling within Algeria.
- Algerians needing temporary transportation.
- Customers renting for weekends, holidays, weddings, or business trips.
- Young professionals who value fast mobile booking.
- Customers comparing models and daily rental prices.

## Core conversion
Reservation/contact request via WhatsApp or configurable contact CTA.

## Brand personality
Modern, reliable, premium, straightforward, local, confident.

## Business model
The website promotes a small sample fleet and turns visitors into rental inquiries.

## Scope
One premium landing page. One-day implementation constraint.

The project does not include booking infrastructure, payments, user accounts, availability APIs, database, or administration.
